Protein import · PEX5L

How Proteins Enter Peroxisomes

Peroxisomes have to import their proteins from the surrounding cell; but how the import machinery builds a route through the membrane is still open. I asked whether human PEX5L complexes form a genuine membrane channel, and how cargo binding changes what that channel does. Protein isolation, membrane reconstitution and electrophysiology, measured directly at the membrane.

Purification · Reconstitution · Electrophysiology Open presentation → Mitochondria · TMBIM5

How Mitochondria Control Calcium Transport

Calcium is one of the cell's loudest signals, and mitochondria help keep it in check. TMBIM5 sits in the inner mitochondrial membrane and had been proposed to carry calcium, so I tested whether it actually conducts. Purification, reconstitution into lipid vesicles, planar bilayer recordings, and gadolinium sensitivity as the deciding experiment.

From Scientific Question to Clinical Trial

Patients with advanced liver cirrhosis are dangerously exposed to bacterial infection, and certain NOD2 variants raise that risk further. Built during my Clinical Research Studies Management training as a mock kick-off for a real Phase III study: design, screening and recruitment, visits and consent, randomization, IMP handling, safety, and who is responsible for what.
